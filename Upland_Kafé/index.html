Our Story

A Roadside Oasis in the Heart of Silang

Upland Kafé is more than just a coffee shop — it's a sanctuary. Nestled along Aguinaldo Highway near Shell Select, our garden-style space invites you to slow down, breathe in the fresh Silang air, and savor every sip.

Whether you're a traveler heading to Tagaytay, a remote worker craving a change of scenery, or friends catching up over pastries — our doors (and garden lights) are always open.
