Our Story

From Family Kitchen
to Your Table

Kusina Box began in a modest kitchen in Brgy. Balite, Silang — where the aroma of simmering adobo and the sound of sizzling sisig filled the air every Sunday. What started as a family tradition of sharing meals with neighbors has grown into a celebration of Filipino culinary heritage.

We source our vegetables from the fertile highlands of Tagaytay and our meats from trusted local suppliers. Every caldereta is slow-cooked for hours. Every sinigang is balanced with the perfect sourness of fresh tamarind.

— The Reyes Family
